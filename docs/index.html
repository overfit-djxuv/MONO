Everything for everyday money

The same app on both phones, with the same rules and the same numbers.

Safe to spend

What is left after bills, planned savings and essentials, with a look-ahead you choose.

Budgets and goals

A monthly budget by category, and savings goals with a plan to reach them.

Subscriptions and bills

Repeating payments you confirm one at a time. Nothing is ever deducted for you.

Accounts and currencies

Several accounts, transfers between them, and exchange rates you enter yourself.

Receipts

Photograph a receipt and it is read on the phone, in English, Spanish or Malay. Burmese is photo plus typing.

Import and backup

Bring in a bank CSV. Encrypted backups made on one phone open on the other.

Private by design

Your records live only on your phone. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to leak.

  • No account, no cloud, no analytics, no ads.
  • The Android app has no internet permission at all, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • Receipts are read on the device. Photos are never uploaded.
  • Backups are yours: a file you keep, optionally locked with a passphrase that never leaves your phone.
  • Open source, so anyone can check.

Get MONO

Android

A signed APK, version 0.5.0. Android 8.0 or newer.

Download MONO-0.5.0.apk
  1. Open the downloaded file on your phone.
  2. When asked, allow “Install unknown apps” for your browser or Files app.
  3. Tap Install, then open MONO.
SHA-256: 51b75343c26375d19f30d3c17416e9daf591153df65019b7776ee09c1a8457a0
Update by installing a newer APK over the old one. Back up first from Tools, Backup and recovery.

iPhone

MONO is not on the App Store. You can build it yourself and install it on your own iPhone for free. You need a Mac with Xcode and a free Apple ID.

Step-by-step on GitHub
  1. Download the source from GitHub.
  2. Run tools/generate.sh and open MONO.xcodeproj.
  3. Pick your team, plug in the iPhone and press Run.
With a free Apple ID the app needs re-signing every 7 days. Your data stays.
